# 📷 VintageCam Android v1.0.0

A modern, high-performance vintage analog camera application for Android built with **Kotlin**, **Jetpack Compose**, **CameraX**, and an **OpenGL ES 3.0 Optical Physics Shader Engine**.

### 🌟 Highlights & Key Features:
- **🎞️ OpenGL ES 3.0 Optical Shader Engine:** Real-time 60 FPS viewfinder with luminance-responsive emulsion grain, anti-halation bloom, and optical lens vignetting.
- **🎨 6 Photochemical Film Stocks:** Kodak Portra 400, CineStill 800T, Fuji Velvia 50, Ilford HP5 Plus (B&W), Retro 1980s Point-and-Shoot, and Standard Glass.
- **🎛️ Samsung One UI Pro Draggable Tick Ruler:** Sleek horizontal draggable tick-mark scale with haptic detents for ISO, Shutter Speed, Focus, and EV.
- **⚙️ Camera2Interop Manual Hardware Controls:** Real ISO stops (50–3200), discrete shutter speeds (1/4000s–1/4s), manual focus distance (Macro to Infinity ∞), and smart EV locking.
- **⚡ Conflict-Free Hardware Flash:** Automatic flash synchronization and LED torch assist for manual exposure captures.
- **📐 Zero-Latency Aspect Ratio Masking:** Instant 3:2, 4:3, and 9:16 optical sensor crop framing without stream dropouts or digital zoom distortion.
- **📅 '90s Quartz Date Stamp:** Incandescent orange datestamp burned cleanly into high-resolution photo exports.
- **🌐 Universal GPU Compatibility:** Strict GLSL ES 3.00 compliance across Qualcomm Adreno (including Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 on Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra), ARM Mali, and PowerVR.

### 📥 Installation:
1. Download **`VintageCam-v1.0.0.apk`** below.
2. Open the downloaded APK on your Android device (Android 8.0+ / Oreo to Android 15+).
3. If prompted, tap **Settings** and allow **Install Unknown Apps**.
4. Launch VintageCam and grant Camera permission.
